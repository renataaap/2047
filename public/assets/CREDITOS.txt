Original rights holders: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier.
Explicit CC0 release: September 2020, as stated in the source OBJ header.
Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
Asset license: CC0 1.0 Universal https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source and full asset legal statement preserved under assets-source/characters/.
The MakeHuman application code is AGPL; none of that code is bundled or linked.

Original adaptations for 2047 Rede Aurora: clothed body materials, woven texture,
workwear pockets/closures, hair, eyes, skeleton and seven in-place animation clips.
These adaptations are also released as CC0 1.0 Universal.
Script: scripts/preparar-personagens.py (Blender 4.3.2).

This is interim character art derived from an anatomical base, not a scanned or
photorealistic final Dante asset. NPCs share the base with individual palettes.
The Fab City People package and the Mixamo Soldier are NOT included.
